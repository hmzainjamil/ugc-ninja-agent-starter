# Asset review checklist

Record the exact asset ID, version, market and placement. Mark each item PASS, FAIL, PENDING or NOT APPLICABLE and link evidence.

- [ ] Product shown matches the approved product and version.
- [ ] Claims match approved wording and supporting evidence.
- [ ] Creator statements are genuine and not fabricated.
- [ ] Synthetic, stock, composite and real footage are correctly classified.
- [ ] Creator release and usage permissions cover the exact placement.
- [ ] Music, logos, locations, fonts and third-party materials are cleared.
- [ ] Required disclosure is visible and understandable.
- [ ] Captions, sound, safe areas and accessibility are checked.
- [ ] CTA and destination are correct and tested.
- [ ] Final asset version is stored and linked to approval.
- [ ] Human approval for the exact final asset is recorded.

A checklist does not grant approval. Any required FAIL or PENDING item blocks release until resolved.
