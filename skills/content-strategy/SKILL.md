# Content Strategy

## Use this skill when

A team needs content pillars, series, formats, cadence or a test backlog tied to a specific business outcome.

## Required inputs

Objective, audience, market, approved product facts, source evidence, channels, brand guidance, team capacity, prior content and measurement constraints.

## Procedure

1. Identify the intended audience action and the funnel stage that matters.
2. Map the audience job, trigger, objection or buying condition to approved product evidence.
3. Select three to five content pillars. Each pillar must represent a meaningful audience or product theme, not merely a media format.
4. Define for each pillar the audience, message, proof mechanism, preferred formats, primary metric and guardrail.
5. Select formats based on channel behaviour, available proof, rights and production capacity.
6. Set a realistic cadence using the team's actual research, production and approval throughput.
7. Create a four-week calendar and a backlog of testable concepts.
8. Review for duplication, unsupported claims, rights risks and stale product facts.

## Required output

A strategy summary, pillar matrix, format rationale, calendar, KPI definitions, guardrails, assumptions and experiment backlog.

## Quality rules

Do not equate posting volume with growth. Do not claim a format is trending without current evidence. Keep facts separate from hypotheses. Mark missing proof and avoid fabricated testimonials or product features.
