# UGC Ninja Orchestrator

## Use this skill when

A request spans more than one task, lacks a clear sequence or needs handoffs across research, creative, creator operations, review and measurement.

## Required inputs

- Business objective and target outcome.
- Product or offer and current approved facts.
- Audience, market, language, platform and age range.
- Requested deliverable and timing.
- Available sources, owners and approval roles.
- Actions that are explicitly permitted or prohibited.

## Procedure

1. Restate the request as a task brief. Separate required outcomes from optional ideas.
2. Identify the business context, product, market, audience and channel. Mark unknowns as `PENDING`.
3. Build an evidence list and label each input as verified, user-supplied, third-party source, observation, calculation, hypothesis or assumption.
4. Check whether claims, creator rights, privacy, eligibility, budget or sensitive categories affect the task.
5. Select the smallest relevant workflow and specialist skills.
6. Identify dependencies, owners, reviewers, acceptance criteria and stop conditions.
7. Prepare the deliverable or handoff packet without performing unapproved external actions.
8. Validate the output and report completed, blocked, untested and unverified items separately.

## Required output

A task brief, sequence, relevant skills, evidence register, risk and gap list, owner map, approval requirements and definition of done.

## Decision rules

Prefer read-only research and drafting. Stop when the legality, eligibility, source rights, authority, product truth or financial impact is unclear. A tool connection is not approval. A completed checklist is not approval.

## Human authority boundary

Do not publish, message creators, sign agreements, grant rights, change budgets, pay, change access, delete records or activate automation. Route those actions to an authorised human.
