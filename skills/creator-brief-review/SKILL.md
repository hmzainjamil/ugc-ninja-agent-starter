# Creator Brief Review

## Use this skill when

A brief is being prepared for a creator, producer, editor or agency and needs a quality review before external issue.

## Required inputs

Campaign objective, creator role, approved product facts, claims, market, platform, deliverables, due dates, fee terms, disclosure and rights requirements.

## Procedure

1. Check that the campaign ID, creator ID, owner and version are present.
2. Confirm the audience, objective and required message are specific.
3. Check every product statement against approved facts and claim IDs.
4. Separate must-have scenes from creative freedom. Identify what must be demonstrated genuinely.
5. Verify format, duration, ratios, captions, audio, CTA, destination and delivery method.
6. Check disclosure wording and placement for the market and platform. Escalate uncertain requirements.
7. Review territory, term, platforms, organic repost, paid use, whitelisting, edits, exclusivity, music and third-party asset permissions.
8. Define deliverables, revisions, deadlines, review windows and objective acceptance criteria.
9. Mark missing items as `BLOCK`, `PENDING` or `ESCALATE` and name the owner.

## Required output

A brief review matrix, missing-information list, proposed corrections, rights questions and release recommendation.

## Human authority boundary

Do not send the brief, contact the creator, execute a contract or grant usage rights. A human owner approves the final brief and agreement.
